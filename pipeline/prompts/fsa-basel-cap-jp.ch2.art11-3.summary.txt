English text: """Products whose purpose of holding falls under any of the following purposes (hereinafter referred to as "trading purposes") shall be classified into the trading account. (i) Short-term resale purpose. (ii) Purpose of obtaining profit from short-term price fluctuations relating to market prices or other indicators. (iii) Purpose of obtaining profit from arbitrage transactions between markets. (iv) Purpose of hedging risks arising from products held for any of the purposes set forth in items (i) through (iii).Among trading products, those other than the following shall be classified into the trading account. (i) Non-listed stocks. (ii) Products planned to be underlying assets for securitization. (iii) Real estate held directly. (iv) Credit extended to individuals or medium and small-sized enterprises (meaning business corporations whose net sales (in cases where such business corporation prepares consolidated financial statements, consolidated net sales; the same applies hereinafter in this item) are less than 5 billion yen; provided, however, that in cases where such business corporation is engaged in wholesale trade or other cases where it is not appropriate to use net sales of such business corporation in judging the scale of its business, those whose total assets are less than 5 billion yen may be included). (v) Equity investments in funds (excluding those set forth in item (ii) of the following paragraph). (vi) Derivative transactions or funds for which any of the products set forth in the preceding items is an underlying asset. (vii) Products held for the purpose of hedging risks of products set forth in the preceding items.Among non-trading products, the following shall be classified into the trading account. (i) Products held for the purpose of market-making business. (ii) Equity investments in funds (limited to those falling under either of the following requirements): (a) The Ultimate Designated Parent Company, etc. can perform look-through (meaning understanding information relating to each individual exposure in the same manner as directly held assets; the same applies in Chapter 6) with respect to the constituent securities of such fund and has obtained sufficient information verified by an independent third party. (b) The Ultimate Designated Parent Company, etc. obtains the market value of such fund on a daily basis and has obtained information relating to the fund's management standards and market risk equivalent amounts. (iii) Listed stocks. (iv) Repo-type transactions relating to trading business. (v) Options.Among non-trading products (excluding those set forth in each item of the preceding paragraph), the following shall be classified into the trading account. (i) Products included in correlation trading portfolios. (ii) Among products with credit risk or equity risk, those constituting any of the following positions: (a) Positions where there is no long position subject to hedging and which are net short positions by individual products or a combination of multiple products. (b) Positions where short positions of products used as hedging instruments against long positions subject to hedging are over-hedged. (iii) Products arising from underwriting business.Unless otherwise provided by laws and regulations regarding the sale of products and hedging of product risks, Ultimate Designated Parent Companies, etc. may classify such products into the trading account.Among products classified into the trading account, those for which fair value measurement is required for accounting purposes shall have their fair value measured on a daily basis and gains or losses on valuation shall be recognized by Ultimate Designated Parent Companies, etc."""
