(i) The amount of shareholders' equity relating to common shares (excluding planned external outflows (meaning planned dividend payments on retained earnings; the same applies hereinafter)). (ii) The amount of accumulated other comprehensive income and other disclosed reserves. (ii-2) The amount of share subscription rights relating to common shares. (iii) The amount of share options relating to common shares. (iv) The amount of adjusted minority interests, etc. relating to Common Equity Tier 1 Capital.In the formula of Article 2, item (i), the amount of adjustment items relating to Common Equity Tier 1 Capital shall be the sum of the following amounts. (i) The sum of the following amounts: (a) The total amount of the following intangible fixed assets: (1) The amount of intangible fixed assets (limited to those relating to goodwill, including goodwill equivalent differences (meaning differences relating to entities that are other financial institutions, etc. (meaning other financial institutions, etc. as provided in Article 8, paragraph (6), item (i)) and that are either insurance subsidiary companies, etc. that are consolidated subsidiaries (meaning consolidated subsidiaries as provided in Article 2, item (iv) of the Consolidated Financial Statement Regulation; the same applies in this (1)) or persons to which the equity method (meaning the equity method as provided in item (viii) of the same Article; the same applies in this (1) and Article 9, paragraph (1)) applies — in the case of insurance subsidiary companies, etc. that are consolidated subsidiaries, amounts displayed included in goodwill pursuant to Article 28, paragraph (5) of the Consolidated Financial Statement Regulation, and in the case of persons to which the equity method applies, amounts equivalent thereto; the same applies in Article 10, paragraph (2), item (vi))). (2) The amount of intangible fixed assets (excluding those relating to goodwill and mortgage servicing rights). (b) The amount of deferred tax assets (excluding those relating to temporary differences). (c) The amount of deferred hedge gains and losses (meaning deferred hedge gains and losses as provided in Article 43-2, paragraph (1), item (ii) of the Consolidated Financial Statement Regulation, excluding gains and losses or fair value differences relating to hedging instruments where the fair value difference attributable to the hedged item is recognized as an item of accumulated other comprehensive income under paragraph (1), item (ii) of the preceding paragraph). (d) In the case of an Ultimate Designated Parent Company adopting the internal ratings-based approach, the amount obtained by deducting the total amount of eligible allowances from the total amount of expected loss amounts (meaning expected loss amounts as provided in Article 126; the same applies in this Chapter) relating to exposures to corporates, etc. and retail exposures, where such total exceeds the total eligible allowances. (e) The amount equivalent to increases in equity capital accompanying securitization transactions. (f) The amount of fair value differences arising from fair value measurement of liabilities (limited to those based on changes in the credit risk of the Ultimate Designated Parent Company or consolidated subsidiary legal entities) that are recognized as equity capital. (g) The amount of assets relating to retirement benefits. (ii) The amount of self-held common shares. (iii) The amount of common shares of other financial institutions, etc. held intentionally. (iv) The amount of common shares of minority-invested financial institutions, etc. (v) The amount exceeding the 10% threshold relating to specific items. (vi) The amount exceeding the 15% threshold relating to specific items. (vii) The amount of Additional Tier 1 Capital shortfall.The "common shares" referred to in paragraph (1) means shares satisfying all of the following requirements. (i) That they are the most subordinated with respect to distribution of residual assets. (ii) That with respect to distribution of residual assets, no fixed amount or maximum amount is stipulated, and that they receive a fair allocation proportional to the number of shares held by the shareholders, after distributions are made to capital instruments having preferential terms. (iii) That they have no maturity date, and are not subject to redemption except in cases based on laws and regulations. (iv) That the issuer has not created any expectation of future repurchase at the time of issuance, and that no terms creating such expectation have been established. (v) That dividends on retained earnings are paid within the range not exceeding the distributable amount calculated pursuant to the provisions of laws and regulations, that the amount thereof is not calculated based on the paid-in amount of shares, and that, except where restricted by the provisions of laws and regulations concerning the distributable amount, no maximum amount is stipulated for dividends on retained earnings. (vi) That the decision to pay dividends on retained earnings can be made at the complete discretion of the issuer, and that refraining from such payment does not constitute a default by the issuer. (vii) That with respect to dividends on retained earnings, they are not preferential relative to other capital instruments. (viii) That, prior to other capital instruments, the issuer bears losses arising to the issuer on a fair basis while continuing operations. (ix) That they are not recognized as liabilities of the issuer in determining whether the issuer is insolvent (meaning a state in which a debtor is unable to fully repay its debts with its assets; the same applies hereinafter) in connection with insolvency proceedings (meaning bankruptcy proceedings, rehabilitation proceedings, reorganization proceedings, or special liquidation proceedings; the same applies hereinafter) of the issuer. (x) That the paid-in amount is accounted for as shareholders' equity under the applicable corporate accounting standards. (xi) That they are currently issued by the issuer, fully paid-in, and that the funds necessary for their acquisition have not been provided, directly or indirectly, by the issuer. (xii) That they are not secured by collateral, and that no terms have been established, whether by the issuer or a person with close ties to the issuer, by way of guarantee or otherwise, that would legally or economically make them preferential over other capital instruments. (xiii) That they have been issued based on a resolution or decision of the general meeting of shareholders, the board of directors, or other body having authority pursuant to laws and regulations. (xiv) That they are clearly distinguished from other capital instruments in the explanatory documents pertaining to the issuer's business year.In calculating the amounts set forth in paragraph (2), item (i), sub-items (a) or (g), where there are amounts of deferred tax liabilities related to such amounts, such amounts and the related deferred tax liability amounts may be offset against each other."""
