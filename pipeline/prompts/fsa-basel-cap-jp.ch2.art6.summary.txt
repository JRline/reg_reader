(b) That the issuer has not taken any action that creates an expectation of redemption or repurchase. (c) That either of the following conditions is satisfied: (1) Where redemption or repurchase is made, capital raising for the purpose of such redemption or repurchase (limited to that ensuring quality equal to or higher than what is being redeemed or repurchased) is carried out prior to or at the time of such redemption or repurchase under conditions recognized as appropriate in light of the issuer's profitability. (2) Even after the redemption or repurchase, it is expected that the issuer will maintain a sufficient level of the Minimum Required Consolidated Capital Adequacy Ratios. (vi) That the issuer does not assume that the confirmation under item (v)(a) will be obtained, and that the issuer has not taken any action that creates an expectation regarding such confirmation.
