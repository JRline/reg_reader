English text: """Notwithstanding the provisions of Articles 11-9 through the preceding Article, the scope of measurement of market risk equivalent amounts in hedge transactions with third parties relating to CVA risk of the Ultimate Designated Parent Company, etc. (hereinafter referred to as "external CVA hedge transactions") and hedge transactions between trading desks and the CVA desk through internal transactions relating to CVA risk (hereinafter referred to as "internal CVA hedge transactions") shall be as provided in the following paragraph and paragraph (3).Among external CVA hedge transactions, those qualifying as eligible CVA hedge transactions (meaning eligible BA-CVA hedge transactions provided in Article 248-3-2 or eligible SA-CVA hedge transactions provided in Article 248-4-13, paragraph (1); the same applies hereinafter in this paragraph and the following paragraph) shall not be included in the scope of measurement of market risk equivalent amounts, and those not qualifying as eligible CVA hedge transactions shall be included in the scope of measurement of market risk equivalent amounts pursuant to the provisions of Chapter 6.Internal CVA hedge transactions must be composed of positions of the CVA desk and positions of trading desks that completely offset each other, and market risk equivalent amounts shall be measured as set forth in each of the following items corresponding to the case set forth in such item. (i) Where internal CVA hedge transactions do not qualify as eligible CVA hedge transactions: Positions of the CVA desk and positions of trading desks shall each be positions subject to measurement of market risk equivalent amounts, and by offsetting both positions, they shall not be included in the scope of measurement of market risk equivalent amounts. (ii) Where internal CVA hedge transactions qualify as eligible CVA hedge transactions: Positions of trading desks shall be included in the scope of measurement of market risk equivalent amounts pursuant to the provisions of Chapter 6.Internal transactions in CVA risk relating to positions subject to capital charges for curvature risk, default risk, or residual risk as defined in the requirements relating to the standardized approach may be taken into account in the measurement of market risk equivalent amounts only where external hedge transactions conducted by the Ultimate Designated Parent Company, etc. with third-party protection providers in the trading account completely offset the positions of such internal transactions.Internal transactions between the CVA desk and trading desks may be used as hedging instruments for the credit equivalent amounts of derivative transactions provided in Article 46, paragraph (1), where the requirements defined in each item of Article 11-10, paragraph (1) are satisfied."""
