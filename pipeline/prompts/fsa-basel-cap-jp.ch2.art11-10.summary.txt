English text: """For risk transfers of credit risk and equity risk from the banking account to the trading account through internal transactions, the hedge effect may be reflected only when the requirements defined in each of the following items corresponding to the category of risk set forth in such item are satisfied. (i) Credit risk: In the trading account, the Ultimate Designated Parent Company, etc. has conducted external hedge transactions (meaning hedge transactions satisfying all of the conditions set forth in each item of Article 94 and each item of Article 96; the same applies hereinafter) with eligible protection providers that are third parties, and has completely offset the positions of internal transactions. (i) Where the requirements defined in each item of paragraph (1) are satisfied: The scope shall include internal transactions and external hedge transactions in the trading account. (ii) Where the requirements defined in each item of paragraph (1) are not satisfied: The scope shall include external hedge transactions in the trading account but shall not include internal transactions in the trading account.
